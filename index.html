About Me

HM

Researcher in Mobility & Transport Modeling

I am a PhD candidate at the Transport Economics Laboratory (LAET), affiliated with the University of Lyon, University Lyon 2, and ENTPE. My research focuses on the economic analysis of carsharing services and their potential role in decarbonizing urban mobility in France.

Under the supervision of Louafi BOUZOUINA and Ouassim MANOUT, I explore how innovative mobility solutions can contribute to sustainable urban development through advanced modeling techniques and economic analysis.

My work combines transport economics, machine learning, and urban planning to understand the dynamics of shared mobility and its environmental impact.

Institution: LAET - Transport Economics Laboratory

University: University of Lyon, Lyon 2, ENTPE

Supervisors: Louafi BOUZOUINA & Ouassim MANOUT

Research Focus

Economic Analysis of Carsharing Services Potential in the Perspective of Urban Mobility Decarbonization in France

Supervisors: Louafi BOUZOUINA & Ouassim MANOUT

This research investigates the economic viability and environmental impact of carsharing services as a solution for reducing carbon emissions in French urban areas. The study employs advanced econometric methods and machine learning techniques to analyze the factors influencing carsharing adoption and usage patterns.
